GCode bundled voicings

The initial 144-chord dataset is generated from original standard movable shapes
by scripts/generate-chords.mjs. It contains no copied third-party chord data.

Optional importer: scripts/import-chords.mjs supports @tombatossals/chords-db
0.5.1 (MIT, Copyright (c) 2016 David Rubert), and copies its complete license
when the imported dataset replaces the bundled dataset.
https://github.com/tombatossals/chords-db
